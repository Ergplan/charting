ergOS Harmony

The ergOS interface kit: tokens, components, charts and screens. Every card below is a live page in this folder; open one to inspect it on its own.

Overview

Cover
Open
Logo The jouleWise wordmark in Harmony glass: Urbanist letters with the “o” as a translucent green glass orb (refraction glow, engraved J-and-wave glyph, specular cap, rim light)

Actions

Button Pill buttons in four weights; the glowing primary is the one thing a screen exists to do.
Open
IconButton Round glass buttons for single-icon actions: back, comment, export, notifications, paging.
Open
SegmentedControl A pill track of 2 to 4 mutually exclusive views: Day view / Month to date, 1 day / 7 days, Timeline / ToD.
Open

Navigation

Sidebar The desktop navigation rail: the ergOS wordmark and role chip, grouped links with 19px icons, the Dashboard group expanded with a tree line, and the organisation at the foot.
Open
TabDock The floating bottom dock of the mobile app: three to four destinations, icon plus one-word label.
Open
TopBar Screen chrome: back button, breadcrumb, then live status, theme, notifications and the user
Open

Data display

ActivityTimeline The trading log: a ruler-ticked rail with round event icons, each event followed by a nested glass card with who, what, block thumbnails and a time pill.
Open
EnergySources The source vocabulary: one icon and one colour per source, used the same way in tiles, chips, legends and chart bands.
Open
FolderCard The signature surface of ergOS: a green glass folder with a raised tab, an S-curved shoulder and frosted sheets tucked behind it
Open
PlantCard A selectable card per plant: name, location, contracted capacity, IEX status and auto-trading state.
Open
ProgressTrack A position along a known span: a solid line for what has happened, a dotted line for what remains, and a downward triangle at "now".
Open
StatTile A glass KPI tile: label and series icon, date, one value with its unit, a 7-day sparkline and a footnote.
Open

Charts

EnergyFlowHarmony The production supply chart (EnergyFlowChart from this repo) in the Harmony theme: the same data engine, toolbar, ToD view, pinned crosshair, CSV and PNG export and linked KPI cards, drawn with Harmony's smooth fading bands, glowing top edges and dotted demand line.
Open
MixRing A thick, gapped ring for the share of each source in today's demand, with the headline share in the centre and a value legend beside it.
Open
PriceExplorer The PriceLandscape and the PriceProfile sharing one store: click any cell in the landscape and the profile below shows that day; step days in the profile and the landscape's outline follows.
Open
PriceLandscape A year of exchange prices in one picture: one column per day, one row per 15-minute block (00:00 at the top), colour from ₹0 to ₹10 per kWh
Open
PriceProfile One day of clearing prices as step lines per block: DAM (bold, glowing), RTM and GDAM, against the state's marginal variable cost (dashed) and the cheapest spare unit's cost (dotted)
Open
ReadoutStrip The legend that reads values: the time of the block in view on the left, then each series with its swatch, value and a note.
Open
SavingsChart Daily net savings as rounded green bars with the cleared volume as a sand line on a second axis, plus a dashed daily average.
Open
SlotHeatmap Seven days by 96 blocks: each cell is one 15-minute block, filled with the IEX series at an opacity proportional to cleared kW
Open
SupplyChart Today's energy by source, block by block: smooth stacked bands for hydro, wind, solar, power exchange and grid under the dotted demand line, a hatched forecast zone after now, and a cursor that rests on the latest block.
Open
TrendChart Two or three overlaid series on one axis, the clean way: a bright series with a glowing line and a deep fade, a dashed series with a quiet grey fade, a cursor with value pills on top and an outlined time pill below
Open
